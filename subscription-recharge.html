Website support is not a subscription

The optional ₹5 website contribution is a one-time payment. It does not establish recurring billing, buy credits, extend access or change the lesson Recharge feature.

Lesson Recharge and paid recharge are different

The current lesson feature called Recharge offers learning breaks, games, puzzles, videos and quiet pauses. It is not a payment or a purchase of credits. These details concern a future paid recharge or subscription only, if one is offered.

Validity, expiry and renewals

No credit-expiry period or subscription duration is announced here. Any validity or expiry condition must be stated in the actual offer before purchase. There is no recurring subscription or automatic renewal offered through the current website. Any future recurring charge requires clear renewal, price and cancellation disclosures and the customer's express authorization.

Transfers, resale and account closure

Transferability, resale restrictions and treatment of purchased benefits when an account closes must be stated with the actual paid offer. Future pricing and existing access

Any future price change must be disclosed before a new purchase or authorized renewal. This page does not change existing learning access, assessment conditions or execution limits, and does not retroactively alter any agreed purchase terms.
