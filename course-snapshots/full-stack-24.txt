class="interview-item"><summary><span>01</span>What is MongoDB?</summary><div class="interview-answer"><p>MongoDB is a document-oriented NoSQL database that stores data as BSON documents organized class="interview-item"><summary><span>04</span>What is ObjectId?</summary><div class="interview-answer"><p>ObjectId is a BSON type commonly used as the value of MongoDB&#39;s _id to have different fields without requiring a traditional fixed relational schema.</p></div></details><details class="interview-item"><summary><span>06</span>What is embedding?</summary><div references?</summary><div class="interview-answer"><p>References are useful when related data is large, independently managed, shared by many documents or has its own
