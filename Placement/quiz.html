Choose by assessment pattern—not only by company name

Your target company or role is not yet fixed.

03

Product Engineering & DSA

Data structures, algorithms, optimisation, edge cases and deeper problem solving.

Examples:

Product engineering and software-development roles with DSA-heavy screening.

Data analyst, AI/ML, data engineering and numerical-programming roles.
