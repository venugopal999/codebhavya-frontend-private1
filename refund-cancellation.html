Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026. Optional website support and future paid services.

Optional ₹5 website support

CodeBhavya offers an optional one-time ₹5 contribution on the Support page when checkout is enabled. It supports website development and does not buy courses, tracer access, mock tests, credits or a subscription. Existing learning and account rules remain the same. Other paid services are still planned for the future; their offer-specific terms will be published before launch.

Current position

You may close the optional contribution checkout before completing payment. There is no recurring contribution to cancel. For an accidental, duplicate or disputed contribution, email support@codebhavya.com with the order or payment reference, amount, date and reason. CodeBhavya will review the payment and request; refunds are not issued automatically by closing the page. This policy does not impose a blanket no-refund condition or a fixed request deadline. Terms for any future paid service will be stated before purchase.

Failed and pending payments

A pending payment requires status confirmation before another attempt. A failed payment with a bank debit may require a reversal through the bank or payment provider. Contact that provider and CodeBhavya support with the reference so the transaction can be checked; a debit alone does not establish a completed purchase.

Duplicate payments and missing access

Report duplicate charges for the same intended purchase with both references. A verified payment without the purchased service also needs investigation. The resolution may involve correcting access, reversing an unintended duplicate charge or providing a refund where due. Consumer rights under applicable law remain protected.

Cancellation and purchased benefits

Any future offer must explain how to cancel and how cancellation affects access or credits before payment. Cancellation of a recurring service and a refund of an earlier charge are separate matters. Refund conditions for consumed services, unused credits and account closure must be disclosed with the actual offer; no restriction is established here by assumption.

Unauthorized transactions

Notify your bank or payment provider immediately if you suspect an unauthorized payment, and contact CodeBhavya support with the transaction reference. Do not share payment credentials or one-time codes. Verification may be needed to resolve a disputed transaction.

Refund method and timing

When a refund is due, it should normally return through the original payment method, subject to payment-provider rules and applicable law. Approved contribution refunds are initiated through Razorpay to the original payment method. Support will communicate the refund reference and provider-estimated timing when available. Bank posting time can differ from refund initiation time; no fixed bank-completion date is promised. Future paid services will disclose their applicable refund details before launch.

How to ask for help

Email support@codebhavya.com with the account email, transaction or order reference, date, amount and reason for the request. Remove sensitive payment details from any screenshot. See the Contact page for other support information.

Consumer rights

Purchase-specific terms cannot remove rights or remedies that applicable law protects. These details supplement the Terms of Use and Payment Terms.
