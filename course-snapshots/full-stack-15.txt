managed by a component or an appropriate state owner that affects rendering.</p></div></details><details class="interview-item"><summary><span>04</span>What is useState()?</summary><div class="interview-answer"><p>useState is a React Hook that lets a functional component store state.</p></div></details><details class="interview-item"><summary><span>05</span>What does useState()
