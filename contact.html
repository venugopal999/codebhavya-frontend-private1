Contact CodeBhavya

For learning questions, website problems, content corrections and privacy requests, email support@codebhavya.com.

Report a content or code problem

Include the page URL, program name, input, expected result and actual result. State the browser or compiler if relevant. Screenshots can help, but remove personal details first.

Account and privacy requests

Describe your request and the account email involved. We may need to verify account ownership before accessing, correcting or deleting account data. Never send a password, authentication token or one-time code.

Response expectations

Requests are reviewed as available. There is no guaranteed response time. CodeBhavya is an educational resource and does not provide emergency support.

Payment questions and complaints

Optional one-time website support is available when enabled; other paid services remain planned for later. For support payment questions, future paid services or a payment-related complaint, contact support@codebhavya.com. If reporting a transaction, include its reference, date, amount and the issue. Do not send card details, CVV, passwords, authentication tokens or one-time codes.
